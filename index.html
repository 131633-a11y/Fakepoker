Velvet poker guide

How to play

Make the best five-card poker hand using your two private cards and the five community cards. The best hand wins the pot.

Check keeps you in when no bet is owed. Call matches the current bet. Bet/Raise puts chips in; use the slider to choose the total amount for this action. Fold gives up the hand.

Each hand uses $10/$20 blinds. You and the AI start with $1,000. The AI estimates hand strength and draws, considers pot odds and the street, and sometimes bluffs.

When a hand ends, choose Deal next hand. If either player runs out of chips, use Restart bankroll to start fresh.
